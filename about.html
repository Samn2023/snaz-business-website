About Sam Digital Marketing & Creative Resources Hub

A curated home base for everyone building something creative — one tool, roadmap and resource at a time.

Our story

Built by Sam Nazir, for every creative juggling too many tabs

This hub started from a simple frustration: the best design, marketing and branding resources exist, but they're scattered across hundreds of bookmarks, blog posts and forgotten browser tabs.

Sam Nazir built this platform to bring it all into one organized, beautifully designed home — for business owners, florists, designers, marketers, students, freelancers and agencies who'd rather spend their time creating than searching.

Every resource listed here is reviewed, categorized and tagged by hand — not auto-generated, not a random link dump.
